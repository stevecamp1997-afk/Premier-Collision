Expert Autobody Capabilities

Comprehensive Collision Services

From minor fender benders and hail dent removal to complete structural frame straightening and custom color matching.

Major Collision Repair

Full-scale body reconstruction following structural accidents. We align factory specs to guarantee occupant safety and factory handling.

  • Unibody & Structural Realignment
  • Airbag & Safety Sensor Alignment

Computerized Paint Match

State-of-the-art paint booth technology for flawless color matching. Seamless blending ensures no visible repair lines or finish variance.

  • OEM Paint Code Spectrum Analysis
  • Clear Coat & High-Gloss Finish

Frame & Unibody Straightening

Precision laser measurement equipment pulls and restores heavy chassis bend angles back to exact original manufacturing dimensions.

  • Heavy Frame Pulling Equipment
  • Structural Rigidity Diagnostics

Dent & Hail Damage Repair

Removing door dings, crease dents, and storm hail impact marks cleanly without harming the vehicle's original factory paint coat.

  • Paintless Dent Removal (PDR)
  • Hail & Acorn Impact Restoration

Bumper & Fender Repair

Plastic bumper welding, crack repairs, bracket replacement, and panel align adjustments to avoid buying costly full component assemblies.

  • Plastic Bumper Weld & Refinish
  • Grill & Lamp Fitting Replacement

Insurance Claim Assistance

  • Direct Insurance Billing
